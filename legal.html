Music

No music is hosted on this site. Every song plays inside YouTube's own embedded player, so YouTube counts the play and handles any payment to rights holders. Songs are linked to YouTube uploads, preferring the official artist and label channels.

By pressing play you are using YouTube, and you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Copyright and takedown requests

If you own something shown here and want it removed or credited differently, open an issue on the project's GitHub issues page. It will be removed as soon as the report is seen.

You can also send a formal notice through GitHub's DMCA takedown process, since the site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, sign-ups, forms, analytics, tracking or ads, and it sets no cookies of its own.

  • Settings on your device. Your volume, last station and background are saved in your browser's local storage under the keys vcr:vol, vcr:st and vcr:bg. They never leave your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them.
  • Offline copy. The site's own files (pages, logos, images and fonts) are cached on your device so it opens quickly with a weak signal. Clearing this site's data deletes the cache too.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address when you visit and may log it for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • YouTube. So music starts quickly on slow connections, YouTube's player loads as soon as the page opens. It loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, youtube-nocookie.com. Google then receives your IP address and device details, and YouTube may store data in your browser while a video plays. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage Google's use of your data at Google security settings.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.
  • Keyboard and gamepad input is handled in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

For privacy questions, use the GitHub issues page.
